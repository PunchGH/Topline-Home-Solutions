## Reliable Home Attic Company for Ottawa, ON & Calgary, AB Residents

Your attic shapes the comfort of every room below it, and TopLine Home Solutions keeps that space working for you. As a dedicated home attic company serving Ottawa, ON & Calgary, AB, we combine insulation, air sealing, and roof protection into a single smart plan for your home. This integrated approach means your home stays cozy in winter and cool in summer, leading to lower energy bills and fresher indoor air. We provide lasting protection, real comfort, and genuine savings with results that hold up for years.

### Attic Insulation

High-performance insulation keeps your home’s temperature steady all year. Your heating and cooling system works less, helping to lower your energy bills. Enjoy a quieter, more comfortable home from top to bottom.

### Air Sealing & Ventilation

Sealing hidden leaks and cracks in your home stops uncomfortable drafts. Proper ventilation improves air quality by moving stale air out and bringing fresh air in. This makes your heating and cooling system run more efficiently.

[Learn More](https://www.toplinehome.ca/services/air-sealing-ventilation/)

Our Services

### Cellulose & Soundproofing

Eco-friendly cellulose insulation adds a thick thermal barrier to your walls and attic. It’s made from recycled paper and blocks outside noise, giving you a quieter, more fire-resistant home.

[Learn More](https://www.toplinehome.ca/services/cellulose-soundproofing/)

Our Services

### GoNano Roofing Spray

This advanced waterproof coating protects your roof from harsh storm damage. Applying this protective layer can add years to your roof’s lifespan, acting as a strong defense against rain, wind, and sun damage.

### Mold Removal & Prevention

Moisture in your attic can lead to mold growth, affecting your indoor air quality. We find and treat the source of the mold to clean the air, and set up prevention measures to keep mold from returning.
