## Create a Quieter, More Energy-Efficient Home Today

Cellulose [insulation](https://www.toplinehome.ca/services/attic-insulation/) is a smart, eco-friendly material made from recycled paper fibers. It's treated for fire and mold resistance, making it durable and safe for your home. Crews pack the dense material into walls, ceilings, and floors, filling every gap to create a sound barrier. This density traps noise and softens sounds between rooms. At the same time, it improves energy efficiency, keeping your home comfortable year-round while lowering energy bills. It’s one practical solution for a quieter, more efficient home.

## A Seamless Seal for Maximum Soundproofing

### Superior Noise Reduction

Cellulose insulation easily absorbs airborne sounds, such as voices and traffic noise, reducing sound transfer between rooms.

### Complete Coverage

This material fills every gap around pipes, wiring, and framing, eliminating hidden paths for sounds and settling snugly to ensure the [area is sealed](https://www.toplinehome.ca/services/air-sealing-ventilation/) with no air pockets.

### Improved Indoor Comfort

Enjoy peaceful rooms as outside noise stays out and privacy improves, while stopping vibrations from traveling between walls and floors in your home.

### Thermal Insulation

Keep your home comfortable year-round with better temperature control. Cellulose insulation helps improve your home’s energy efficiency while saving you money.
